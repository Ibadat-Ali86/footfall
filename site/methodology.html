Methodology Specification (m1.0)

Rigorous mathematical definitions, lifecycle classification rules, robust normalization algorithms, and anomaly defenses governing Footfall measurements.

1. Core Mathematical Metrics

All metrics are computed weekly on calendar-week boundaries (Monday 00:00 UTC) with zero lookahead leakage:

1. Weekly Downloads (W): Aggregated package manager installs (npm + PyPI) for the 7-day observation window.
2. Log-Ratio Velocity (L): L = ln((W_t + 1) / (W_{t-4} + 1))
3. Four-Week Growth (g_4): g_4 = (W_t - W_{t-4}) / max(W_{t-4}, 100)
4. Twelve-Week Growth (g_12): g_12 = (W_t - W_{t-12}) / max(W_{t-12}, 100)
5. Star Velocity (s_28): 28-day net organic star gain, adjusted for burst exclusions.
6. Adoption z-score: (L - Median(L_category)) / (1.4826 * MAD(L_category))
7. Attention z-score: (s_28 - Median(s_category)) / (1.4826 * MAD(s_category))
8. Divergence (D): D = attention_z - adoption_z

2. Table-Driven Lifecycle Classifier

Evaluated on a strict first-match-wins basis. Every rule is deterministic, parameterized, and reproducible.

Priority Phase Mathematical Criteria Economic Interpretation
1 insufficient_data History < 12 weeks or W < 100 Initial gestation or uncalibrated baseline.
2 breakout g_4 ≥ 0.50 AND adoption_z ≥ 1.00 AND W ≥ 1,000 AND D ≤ 0.50 Rapid organic adoption velocity confirmed by package installs.
3 attention_ahead D > 1.00 AND s_28 ≥ 500 AND W < 5,000 High social awareness/hype exceeding installed production usage.
4 use_ahead D < -1.00 AND W ≥ 5,000 AND adoption_z > 0.00 Workhorse utility; high deployment volume with low social chatter.
5 fading g_12 < -0.20 AND adoption_z < -0.50 Persistent multi-quarter contraction in package manager usage.
6 emerging W < 5,000 AND g_4 > 0.20 Early-stage adoption momentum within initial developer cohorts.
7 mainstream W ≥ 25,000 AND |g_12| ≤ 0.20 Established ecosystem fixture with stable deployment volume.
8 plateau Default fallback Steady-state mature operation without significant acceleration.

3. Anomaly & Manipulation Defenses

  • Star Burst Detection: Scans stargazer timestamps using a Poisson process threshold. Bursts exceeding 5x Poisson expected rate within a 72-hour window are excluded from organic star velocity.
  • Download Step Detection: Rolling 26-week median and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) identify single-day artificial spikes (|z| ≥ 4.0), preventing CI runner storms from distorting scores.
  • Neutral Language Standard: In compliance with ethics rules, anomalies are labeled objectively as "unusual velocity" and never use accusatory phrasing.
