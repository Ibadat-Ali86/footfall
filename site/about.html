About Footfall

Footfall is an independent, open-source measurement system providing empirical developer adoption analytics for AI frameworks, SDKs, vector stores, and MCP servers.

Governance: Public Scientific Methodology License: Apache-2.0 / CC BY 4.0 Security Contact: security@footfall.dev Disputes Contact: disputes@footfall.dev

Core Principles & Ethics

  • Aggregates Only: We store zero personal data. No GitHub usernames, stargazer profiles, or contributor identifiers are ever recorded in our databases or ledgers.
  • No Negative Badges: Maintainer README badges never display critical labels. Only positive milestones (breakout, use_ahead, mainstream) are shown; otherwise a neutral tracked badge is emitted.
  • Objective Language: We never accuse projects of fraud or fake metrics. Statistical outliers are objectively described as "unusual velocity" with full contextual data.
  • Zero Commercial Conflicts: Footfall is not an investment fund, does not sell consulting ratings, and does not accept paid placements.

Maintainer Dispute Process

If your repository displays an unusual star activity or download surge flag, you have an immediate right of reply:

  1. Email disputes@footfall.dev or open an issue on GitHub using our dispute template.
  2. Provide links documenting external organic exposure (e.g., product launch, Hacker News submission, conference demo).
  3. Our team acknowledges within 48 hours and masks the flag with a neutral "disputed" notice during review.
  4. Final empirical determinations are issued within 7 business days and logged in our public repository.

Privacy & Data Minimization Summary

Footfall adheres to strict data minimization principles:

  • Zero Trackers: This website contains zero third-party scripts, zero analytics beacons, and zero tracking cookies.
  • Strict Content Security Policy: All resources are served exclusively from this origin (default-src 'self').
  • Edge Rate Limiting: IP addresses processed at the edge for rate limiting (60 req/min) are hashed with a rotating salt and retained for at most 14 days.
